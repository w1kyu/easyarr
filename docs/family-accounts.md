# Family accounts

The account you create during setup is **your** account. It's the admin account for:

- **Jellyfin** — where you watch movies, shows and music.
- **Jellyseerr** — where you request new things (it logs in with your Jellyfin account).

The other apps inside EasyArr don't need a login of their own: they are protected by your
Umbrel login.

## Accounts for family and friends

Everyone in your home can get their own Jellyfin account. Then everybody has their own
"continue watching" list, and you can choose what each person may watch or request.

You create those accounts in Jellyfin after the setup is finished. A step-by-step guide will
appear here in a later version of EasyArr.

## Forgot your password?

You can set a new one in EasyArr's Settings (coming in a later version).
